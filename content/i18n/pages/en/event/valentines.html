VALENTINE’S2026

A two-week party rare-drop event: party RDR and shared Daily Forecast rules

Valentine’s is a smaller, two-week Ephinea event with no exclusive quests, currency, shops or event NPCs. It encourages four-player parties through fixed party-size bonuses and shared Daily Forecasts that increase RDR.

Party rare drop rate

Party-size bonus

The fixed bonus depends on the current party size; four players receive +20% RDR.

+0%1 player
+5%2 players
+10%3 players
+20%4 players
Forecast sharing

How Daily Forecast sharing works

Each player contributes the Daily Forecast bonuses matching their class, race and gender that day. Contributions add together for up to another +20% RDR.

Fixed party-size bonus

Only the current party size matters, regardless of character type. A four-player party gets +20%.

Additional Forecast bonus

Every member’s active Forecast is shared with the whole party and stacks with the party-size bonus.

No exclusive drops

There is no Valentine’s currency, limited prize pool or exclusive item, so no exchange NPC is needed.

No exclusive quests

The current event has no Ephinea-exclusive Valentine’s quest. The old SEGA quests recorded in 2016 belong to the historical event.

For the best bonus, form a four-player party, then choose classes, races and genders to match the day’s Forecast. Together, the two bonuses can provide up to +40% RDR.
