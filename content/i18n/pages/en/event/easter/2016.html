First Ephinea egg hunt

Easter Event 2016

The first event introduces Event Eggs as a separate currency dropped by enemies everywhere, with an account-based Egg discovery leaderboard.

19 March–9 AprilThree weeksTop 10 leaderboard
  • Any enemy capable of normal drops can drop Event Eggs; fleeing Rappies cannot.
  • Event Eggs cannot be used directly; they are reserved for exchanges or gambles in the later Egg Shop.
  • The server records each account’s Egg discoveries. The top ten receive special rewards at the end.
