How the Christmas event works

Ephinea’s Christmas event usually lasts around four weeks. Defeat enemies for presents, clear densely populated event quests for , then exchange it for more presents at the Christmas Event Shop. prize pools change from year to year.

1Defeat enemies and run event questsNormal enemy kills in every episode and difficulty can drop presents. Boxes and fleeing Rappies cannot.
2Collect presents and Use a to receive a random item. Event quests such as Christmas Fiasco award on completion.
3Visit the event shopSelect Episode 1 → Shop → Christmas Event Shop. Exchange for more presents with The Hunter near the bank.
4 types depend on difficultyN / H / VH drop Common Presents; Ultimate drops Ultimate Presents. They look identical but have different prize pools.
