2019 · 4th Anniversary Event

The fourth anniversary activated all weekly boosts from the start and restricted milestone counts to Ultimate.

2 August–6 September4th AnniversaryAll weekly boosts active together
Changes from 2018
  • 4th Anniversary Badges introduced; 1st and 2nd Badges retired.
  • Only Ultimate kills count towards this year’s milestones.
  • The normal weekly rotation is disabled, with all four boosts active from the start.
  • Hunter offers only novelty weapons; shop stock and prices were revised.
