What changed

Eleven changes for the 11th anniversary

Quests and currency remain unchanged for now. The main changes concern badge distribution, collection, milestones and the Gold Shop.

Separate badge rolls

Badges no longer occupy an enemy’s normal drop slot. An enemy can drop both an ordinary item and a badge.

One result for the party

Each enemy rolls for a badge once, and party members receive the same badge from that enemy.

Classic drops exception

In multiplayer parties using Classic drops, successful badge drops appear on the ground.

Automatic deposits for enemy drops

Badges go into the character or shared bank, falling to the ground only if there is no space.

Deposit notifications

Automatic deposits are shown on the right of the screen. Each character can toggle notifications with /badgenotify .

Rare-drop sounds retained

Automatically deposited badges still play the rare-drop sound if it is enabled in the Ephinea .

Floating ground badges

Badges on the ground bob gently, making them easier to distinguish from other red boxes.

Automatic deposits for clear rewards

MAE and AA clear-reward badges go into the character or shared bank, or into the inventory if neither has space.

Reordered milestones

Milestones have been reordered and slightly expanded, while remaining concentrated so each unlock provides a larger overall reward.

Revised targets

This year’s milestone targets are estimated from previous anniversary events.

Two new Gold Shop items

and have been added at 99 Gold each.

Badge economy

The four badge tiers remain

Badges can drop from enemies in all areas, not just event quests. They are more common at higher difficulties.

BronzeAll difficulties

Basic shop purchases and Thief gambles; exchange 10 Bronze for 1 Silver.

SilverVery Hard · Ultimate

Materials, novelty weapons and Paint; exchange 10 Silver for 1 Gold.

GoldUltimate

High-tier fixed exchanges and random untekked weapons from Hunter.

PlatinumUltimate · Extremely rare

Give one to Guide to choose a top-tier reward.

Normal drop-rate modifiers and party size do not affect badge rates. Rappies that are scared away rather than killed do not drop badges.

Deferred redesign

The anniversary redesign is postponed, not cancelled

The following plans come from the 2025 redesign announcement and are not the live 2026 rules.

QuestEvent MAE will be retired

Permanent MAE quests will remain, and August Atrocity will still return for anniversaries. New event quests will have similar difficulty, without a Forest-only entry-level route.

CurrencyMultiple Anniversary Tokens

Each selected quest will award its own Token. Shop purchases will require combinations of Tokens, encouraging participation in all event quests.

TradingTokens will be tradable

Players can trade Tokens from their preferred quests instead of running every quest themselves. Colours and quantities in the official examples are not final.

Equivalent rare Platinum Badge rewards, server milestones and unlockable boosts will remain. Existing anniversary badges will stop dropping, but their exchange quest will stay available.
