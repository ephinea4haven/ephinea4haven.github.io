    Core gameplay
    Badges still drop across all areas. MAE forms the anniversary HBR and the main kill-count quests. Find the shop at Normal → EP1 → Shop; Guide shows personal counts.
    From this year onward, this site uses the names Anniv. Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum Badge for subsequent years.
