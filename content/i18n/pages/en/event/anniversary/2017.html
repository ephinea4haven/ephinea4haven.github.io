2017 · 2nd Anniversary Event

Officially renamed Anniversary Event, with Maximum Attack E, badges and server-wide kill milestones forming its annual core.

13 August–10 September2nd AnniversaryMAE becomes the main quest series
This year’s core features
  • 2nd Anniversary Badges introduced; older badges can be converted at a reduced ratio.
  • Four badge types: Bronze on all difficulties, Silver on VH/U, Gold and Platinum on U.
  • Maximum Attack E becomes the anniversary-exclusive quest series; normal weekly boosts stop rotating during the event.
  • MAE and Christmas Fiasco kills advance server milestones; lower difficulties have character-level requirements for counting.
    • Badge conversion10 Bronze = 1 Silver；10 Silver = 1 Gold
    Quest featuresMAE takes around 5–10 minutes and cannot fail
    Personal progressAsk Guide in the anniversary shop
    Server milestones
    All 11 MAE quests and Christmas Fiasco count, but Normal, Hard and Very Hard require levels 31, 51 and 101 respectively. The final server kill count was 6,092,971. Ask Guide in the anniversary shop for personal progress.
