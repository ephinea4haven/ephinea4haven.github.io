2020 · 5th Anniversary Event

The fifth anniversary kept MAE central to HBR and milestones, with all weekly boosts active together from the start.

17 August–14 September5th AnniversaryMAE HBR
Changes from 2019
  • 5th Badges replaced 4th Badges; 3rd and earlier badges were retired.
  • Only Ultimate kills count towards milestones, and MAE is the current Hunter’s Boost Road.
  • The normal weekly rotation is disabled; all four boosts are active from the start.
  • Hunter still offers only novelty weapons. Shop prices and stock were revised again.
