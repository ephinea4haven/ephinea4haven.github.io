2024 · 9th Anniversary Event

The ninth anniversary introduced August Atrocity with enemies from multiple episodes, and changed milestones into a server-wide HBR-style system.

12 August–9 September9th AnniversaryAugust Atrocity debuts
Changes from 2023
  • August Atrocity #1 and #2 were added under Event in EP1 and EP2 respectively.
  • Both AA quests use omnispawn, mixing enemies from other areas and even other episodes.
  • Milestones now count Ultimate MAE and AA clears.
  • The least-cleared event quest determines the server milestone, much like a server-wide Hunter’s Boost Road.
    • MAE11 short quests, 5–10 minutes each, with no failure conditions
