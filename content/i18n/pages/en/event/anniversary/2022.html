2022 · 7th Anniversary Event

The seventh anniversary restored the normal weekly boost rotation and rebalanced MAE, shops and milestone requirements.

Changes from 2021
  • and were removed from the Gold Shop.
  • joined the Gold Shop; Scavenger was removed.
  • MAE clear-reward Meseta was halved.
  • The four weekly boosts no longer run together; the normal rotation returns during the event.
  • Festivity on the Beach is available from the start. This is Chef’s final appearance.
  • Milestone targets were extended, but personal minimum kills and HBR point requirements were removed.
