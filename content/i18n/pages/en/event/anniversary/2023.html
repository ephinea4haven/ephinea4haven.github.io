2023 · 8th Anniversary Event

The eighth anniversary moved from HBR to RBR. For the first time, milestones counted all Ultimate kills across the server.

5 August–5 September8th AnniversaryThe RBR era
Changes from 2022
  • Every monster defeated on Ultimate counts towards milestones, not just those in Maximum Attack E.
  • Boost Road is now RBR. MAE is no longer a fixed Boost Road selection, and RBR continues its usual random rotation.
  • dropped from 99 to 95 Gold.
  • Seven new Paints joined the Silver Shop at 99 Silver each.
  • Milestone rewards were increased after HBR was removed. All four weekly boosts run simultaneously during the event.
