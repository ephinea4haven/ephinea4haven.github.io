What changed

Five changes for the 10th anniversary

Rather than simply adding rewards, this year uses dynamic modifiers to direct players towards the quests furthest behind in server progress.

AA is no longer a required clear

August Atrocity is no longer among the quests that must each be cleared for milestones.

Double progress per clear

An AA clear contributes twice to the current lowest MAE clear count.

Dynamic ±25%

Once the gap exceeds 500 clears, the bottom three receive a bonus and the top three a penalty.

More concentrated milestones

Fewer unlocks, but larger rewards per unlock and higher score targets.

Badge economy

Four badge tiers

Badges drop from enemies throughout the game, not just event quests. Boxes and fleeing Rappies do not drop them.

Bronze Normal and above

Basic shops and Thief gambles; upgrade 10 to 1 Silver.

Silver Very Hard · Ultimate

Materials, novelty weapons, Item Tickets and .

Gold Ultimate

High-tier fixed exchanges and Hunter’s random untekked weapons.

Platinum Ultimate · Extremely rare

Give one to Guide to choose one top-tier reward.

Ordinary DAR, RDR and party size do not affect badge rates; only anniversary-specific modifiers do.
