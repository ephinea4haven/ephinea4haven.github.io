This year's changes

Cookies adopt the 2026 Anniversary Badge drop changes, including the multiplayer Classic drops exception.

One roll for the whole party

Each eligible monster makes one Cookie drop roll for the entire party. When the roll yields a Cookie, every player receives one. The Cookie roll is independent of enemy drops, so an enemy can yield its usual drop and a Cookie.

Automatic bank deposits

Cookies obtained on Ragol go into the character or shared bank. They fall to the ground when neither bank has space. Multiplayer Classic drops follow the exception below.

Deposit notifications

Use /eventnotify to toggle automatic deposit notifications per character. Under the anniversary system, automatic deposits still play the rare drop sound if enabled in the launcher.

Preferred bank

Use /eventbankpref to choose which bank receives priority.

Shared anniversary settings

The new commands use the same server settings as /badgenotify and /badgebankpref. The old commands are due to be removed soon; use the new generic commands.

More visible ground drops

Cookies on the ground bob up and down like Anniversary Badges, making them easier to spot among overlapping drops.

Multiplayer Classic drops exception

Under the same anniversary system, Cookies go to the floor when a multiplayer party uses Classic drops. This is a party drop style, distinct from the Classic ship.
