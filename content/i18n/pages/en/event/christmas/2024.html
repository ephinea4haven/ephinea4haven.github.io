Christmas Event 2024

Event dates: 14 December 2024–11 January 2025

Rules for this year
  • Normal, Hard and Very Hard share a pool; Ultimate Presents have a separate tiered pool.
  • All weekly boost effects double during the event. rates double on 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January.
  • Christmas Fiasco is available in Episodes 1, 2 and 4, awarding 4, 6 and 6 respectively.
  • Enemies on any difficulty can drop presents. Boxes and fleeing Rappies cannot, but normally defeated Rappies can.
  • rates ignore DAR and daily or weekly DAR/RDR bonuses.
  • St Rappy replaces Love Rappy in VR Temple and drops a different containing Mag Cells.
