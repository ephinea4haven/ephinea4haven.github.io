EASTER2026

Four weeks of hunting: drop rates, RBR, the Egg Shop and its three vendors

Easter is a major event lasting around four weeks. Normal enemies can drop Event Eggs. Take them to Episode 2 → Shop → The Egg Shop for fixed exchanges or gambles with three NPCs.

rates

Base drop rates

Boxes, fleeing Rappies and enemies with 0% DAR cannot drop Event Eggs.

Since 2024, RBR quests boost rates by +15% for one player, +20% for two and +25% for three or four. Other server drop-rate modifiers and regular boosts do not affect Event Eggs.
The Egg Shop

Three shop NPCs

The quest is available all year, but the Check Room is unavailable. Withdraw the Event Eggs you want to spend before entering.

Elly · Fixed exchanges

Materials, novelty weapons, Easter Eggs, Item Tickets, Mag Cells and rare equipment, priced from 1 to 60 Eggs.

Karen · TypeM weapons

TypeM weapons with fixed special attacks and Hit cost 35 Eggs each, including TypeGU, TypeME and TypeSH.

Male NPC on the left · Egg gamble

The 1-Egg gamble always wins; 5 Eggs has a 50% chance and 10 Eggs a 10% chance. More expensive gambles offer rarer prizes on success.

Female NPC at the back · Return to lobby

Ends The Egg Shop and returns you to the lobby; she does not handle exchanges.
