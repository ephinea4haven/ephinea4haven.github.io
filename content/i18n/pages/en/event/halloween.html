HALLOWEEN2025

Two weeks of Cookie hunting: Halloween quests, quest boosts and Hallo Rappy prizes

Halloween is a smaller event lasting around two weeks. Halloween Cookies drop throughout the server. One Cookie per party starts a densely populated Halloween quest; these quests are available all year but require an entry item.

Cookie rates

Base drop rates

Cookies are unaffected by ordinary drop-rate boosts. During the event, quests in the Halloween category provide a +20% bonus.

Quest activation

How to use Cookies

Only one Cookie is needed for the party. Any member can give it to the NPC near the teleporter to start the quest for everyone.
