Equipment Technique boosts

Certain weapons, frames and barriers boost specific Techniques. Attack Techniques usually gain damage; healing and support Techniques usually gain range. Equipment boosts to Megid grant piercing, not a higher instant-kill chance.

Applicable percentage boosts stack additively. For example, a character with an innate 30% Foie boost who equips an item granting another 30% Foie boost has a total boost of 60%.

The data below follows Ephinea PSO Wiki’s Equipment-based boosts reference.
