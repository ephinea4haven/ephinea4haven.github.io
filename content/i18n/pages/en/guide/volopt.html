The Vol Opt form 1 Monitor damage bug

Vol Opt's first form has a damage transfer bug: when you hit a Monitor with an attack technique (other than Megid) or a physical attack, the damage passes to the core in an unusual way. The transfer formula is:

Damage = (ATP - DFP) / 5

In this formula:

  • the weapon's Machine % counts towards effective ATP as usual;
  • the global 0.9 damage factor is not applied;
  • elemental resistance, cane boosts and technique power make no difference.

With this bug, equipping a high-ATP weapon and casting Gizonde to lock Vol Opt in place defeats the first form very efficiently. Each Gizonde hit causes a brief stun, so casting it repeatedly keeps the boss unable to act (Gizonde stunlock).

Choosing a weapon

The table covers several common weapon setups. It assumes the character's ATP is maxed and a (+35 ATP) is equipped:

  • ⚔ Excalibur and Galatine: high-ATP swords, the most common choice
  • ★ Sacred Duster and Angry Fist: fist weapons; male characters' bare-handed casting animation is the fastest, making the stun rhythm easier to keep
  • ◆ Red Saber + Crimson Coat (CC) and Orotiagito + Samurai Armor (SA): set bonuses add extra ATP
  • ⚔ Vivienne: an option for FOnewearl
