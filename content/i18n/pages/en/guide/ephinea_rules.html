Game and forum rules

Your use of the forums and game server is subject to the rules on this page, which apply everywhere. Not knowing or not reading these rules is no excuse for breaking them.

You are responsible for reading and understanding the rules. Questions about them can be put to the staff for clarification.

By using the forums and game server, you agree to the following:

No harassment, deliberately offensive content or hate speech. Publicly naming, defaming or belittling other users is not allowed, nor is posting content solely to anger or offend other users.

Stalking, sexual harassment, or any form of discriminatory hate speech against other players will result in immediate action on your account.

No illegal content. Don’t post links to, or discuss, things that don’t belong on the board, including but not limited to distributing illegal files such as ROMs, ISOs, cracks and warez.

No spam. Spamming the forums or anywhere else will lead to action on your account. Keep the forums readable, and edit your posts when needed rather than making new ones.

Don’t cheat. If you are found cheating outside Sandbox mode, you will be immediately and permanently banned. If we believe you are using an unfair system, staff will warn you first, and continued use will lead to action on your account.

No multi-boxing (using several computers) to abuse the PSO2 drop style for extra drops. If you need to use several computers and don’t want to risk a ban, use the classic drop style.

Scanning other players’ inventories, or using any tool that lets you see other players’ inventories, is not allowed; any evidence that you have done so will get you banned immediately.

No discussing RMT (real money trading), and no public RMT of any kind. Discussing any RMT will lead to action on your account. You may not engage in RMT with other users,

nor discuss with other users doing so or having done so, nor talk about other players allegedly involved in RMT. Action will be taken on your account.

Staff have the final say on the rules. If a staff member tells you to stop doing something, stop. Claiming that we are suppressing your free speech,

or that these rules don’t list the offence, is a quick way to get action taken on your account. Ignoring warnings from staff will also lead to action on your account.

The issues above may not be discussed publicly. If you think a GM unfairly warned or banned you, complain to that staff member. If you’d rather not deal with that GM, feel free to contact another GM.

Discussing it publicly will lead to further action on your account. Player violations are not a matter for other users to discuss; they are the GMs’ business only.

Using the forums is a privilege, not a right. In extreme cases your account may be banned indefinitely, without warning.

That’s all!
