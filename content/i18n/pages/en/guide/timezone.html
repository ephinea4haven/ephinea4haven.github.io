Why pay attention to time zones?

The Ephinea server was built and is run by Sodaboy, an American enthusiast, and its players are spread across every time zone. PSOBB has no auction house or similar trading system, so trades mostly happen on the official forums or Discord; knowing the time zones helps you arrange trade times with other players more efficiently.

Useful time zone tools:

  • World Time Buddy — compare several time zones to arrange trade times
  • Time.is — check the exact current time
  • World Time Zone — an overview map of world time zones
