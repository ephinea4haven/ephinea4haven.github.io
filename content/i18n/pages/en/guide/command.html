Server commands

On the Ephinea server you can type commands in game to customize your experience and use specific features.

Command list ##

/lang x

Sets the quest language: 1 for English, 2 for Japanese.

/setname x

Sets the party name, up to 12 characters.

/setpass x

Sets the party password, up to 16 characters.

/partyinfo

Shows the current party’s information:

  • Experience Rate
  • Experience Share Settings
  • Section ID
  • Drop Style
  • Drop Rate
  • Rare Enemy Rate

/rbr

Shows a scrolling announcement at the top of the screen with this week’s RBR quests.

/luck

Shows your current character’s daily forecast luck level: Normal, Good, Lucky or Incredible.

/forecast

Shows today’s daily forecast conditions.

/noluck

Toggles whether your current character accepts the daily forecast’s rare drop rate bonus.

/purist

Typing /purist in game turns on Sage mode, which isn’t an account mode (like Normal or Sandbox).

EphineaThe server periodically gives players drop boosts; if you want the original drops, you can use Sage mode’s base drop mode.

Note that during Sage mode, joining another room with a rare enemy drop boost still gives you that boost, but players who join your room get no drop boosts.

/bank

Switches characters and the shared bank while logged in. Note: there is only one shared bank, used by all 32 characters.

/lobby

Returns to the lobby.

/showwep

Whether to show special weapon pickup messages, including weapons in your inventory.

/matcount

Shows your current character’s material usage in the announcement bar at the top of the screen.

/matreset

Resets your current character’s material usage, not including HP and TP Materials,

. Only usable in the lobby, and must be entered twice to confirm.

/killcount

Shows all your unsealing counts, including Lame d’Argent, Sealed J-Sword, Limiter, Swordsman Lore and others, in the announcement bar at the top of the screen.

/wipecmode

Clears your current character’s CH data.

/modname x

Changes your character’s name, up to 10 characters; the Section ID is not recalculated. No limit on use.

/modsecid x

Changes your character’s Section ID; usable once, before level 20.

Valid values of x:

**VIRIDIA, GREENILL, SKYLY, BLUEFULL, PURPLENUM,

PINKAL, REDRIA, ORAN, YELLOWBOZE, and WHITILL**

/ping

Pings the server to check latency. Because of how PSO works, the minimum ping is 66ms.

/time

Shows the server’s current time (UTC; add 8 hours for China).

/sfx 0-10

Use /sfx 0-10 to adjust the sound effect volume.

/bgm 0-10

Use /bgm 0-10 to adjust the background music volume.

/cam

Turns PSO’s default camera behaviour on or off; when off, the left mouse button moves forward and the right mouse button looks around in place.

/map

Turns the large map in the centre of the screen on or off.

/gamefilter

Shows rooms you can join.

/slowburst

Enables loading screens for all players in the current room (room leader only).

/burstpref

Enables all loading screens for you in the lobby and in parties you create.

/mobhp

Turns enemy HP bars on or off.

/resetmobhp

Resets the enemy HP bar settings.

/mobhpfilled

Sets the colour of a full enemy HP bar.

/mobhpfilling

Sets the colour of an enemy’s remaining HP.

/mobhpdamage

Sets the colour of an enemy’s lost HP.

/starttimer x

Starts a simple timer immediately. “x” is an optional number of seconds; the timer counts down from x to 0, or counts up from 0 if not given.

/freezetimer

Stops the current timer. A stopped timer usually can’t be started again.

/killtimer

Destroys the current timer.

/revealtimer

Shows a timer that was accidentally hidden…

/registertimer x,y,z

Immediately creates a timer that waits for y to be set and stops automatically when z is set; if x is set, it counts down from x to 0.

/zonetimer x,y,z,r

Immediately starts a timer that waits for floor y area z to be cleared; if r is set, the timer waits for r to be set before counting; if x is set, it counts down from x to 0.

/pb

Sets your character’s PB to 100 (Sandbox)

/sd

Sets your character’s S/D level, up to 81 (Sandbox)

/sdall

The room leader sets the S/D level of everyone in the room, up to 81 (Sandbox)

/sethp

Sets your character’s HP (Sandbox)
