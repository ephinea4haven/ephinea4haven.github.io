About Ephinea

Ephinea is a free private server for Phantasy Star Online Blue Burst (PSOBB). It keeps SEGA’s original core gameplay and adds modern online systems, conveniences, optional challenge content and long-running events.

What the server aims for

Normal mode keeps the original experience rates, rare enemy rates, class stats and nearly all item parameters by default. Drop tables are adjusted for balance, especially on Ultimate, but iconic items keep the drop sources players know as far as possible. Ephinea sells nothing that affects combat strength and has no server-made equipment.

The goal isn’t to remake PSO but to improve multiplayer cooperation, client compatibility and long-term play on top of the original combat framework. Players who want something closer to GameCube/Xbox Episode I & II can choose the separate Classic mode.

Core online systems

Shared experience

Party members in the same area get full experience without first hitting the enemy, so support, control and clearing all contribute to the party’s growth.

Separate drops

You can use a drop system where each player sees their own drops, so there’s no racing for items; shared drops remain available as a room option.

No damage cancelling

The original Damage Cancel is gone, so teammates attacking together no longer have one hit’s higher damage overwritten by another’s.

Single-player quest support

Nearly every quest can be played in Multiplayer and One Person mode, and doors that need several players have been adapted for solo play.

Character and account conveniences

32 character slots

Each account has 32 character slots and a shared bank every character can access; type /bank to switch banks.

Full dressing room

You can still change your appearance after creating a character, and server features let you change your character’s name and Section ID.

Bigger bank and stacking

The bank holds up to 999,999,999 Meseta, and Materials and Grinders stack to 99 in both your inventory and the bank.

Steady shops and drop alerts

The tool shop always stocks common recovery items; rare items play a sound when they drop and show a red dot on the minimap.

Optional modes and long-running systems

Classic mode

A separate character mode focused on Episode I & II, with Ephinea-specific boosts such as Anguish and RBR turned off, for an experience closer to the early console versions.

Anguish levels

An extra difficulty for Ultimate rooms that raises enemy HP, damage, accuracy, resistances and more, while also raising experience, Meseta and rare drop rewards.

Challenge and Battle modes

Both Challenge and Battle modes are playable; Ephinea also offers Challenge mode on Ultimate with its own character setups.

Sandbox mode

For testing builds and experimenting; commands can create items or adjust characters. Sandbox characters can only party with characters in the same mode.

Ragol Boost Road

Each week one quest from each of three episodes rotates in, giving extra DAR, RDR and experience boosts; playing in a party raises the rewards further.

Rotating boosts and daily forecast

The server rotates experience, DAR, RDR or rare enemy boosts on a schedule; the daily forecast also gives an RDR bonus to specific class, race and sex combinations.

Client and community

Modern display support

The client supports widescreen, a high-res HUD, several Direct3D/Vulkan renderers and optional shaders.

Control improvements

Supports extra shortcut keys, XInput triggers and configurable pickup and Photon Blast controls to reduce mis-presses.

Multiple languages and regional servers

The official client and main quests support English and Japanese, with Ships in different regions to reduce network latency.

Lobbies and leaderboards

Each Block has extended lobbies and a jukebox; the official leaderboards track character growth, combat, wealth and other stats.
