This page gathers the main NPCs of the PSO story (before PSO2), grouped by faction, with how they relate to each other.

The world in brief: The home planet Coral is on the brink of collapse, and humanity sends colony ships Pioneer 1 land on Ragol to scout ahead. Then Pioneer 2 set out with about 30,000 colonists, but shortly after receiving Pioneer 1’s “landed successfully” signal, the planet’s surface was rocked by a massive explosion and all communication was lost. Pioneer 2 begins its investigation from orbit around Ragol—the stage for the Hunters played by the players.

🗡️ Pioneer 1 heroes

HEROES OF PIONEER 1
RR

A legendary hunter and scientist, called “Red Ring Rico” after the signature red ring on her left arm. The daughter of Tyrell and apprentice of Flowen —her Red Ring / Red gear was all a gift from her master. After the explosion on Ragol she left a series of holographic messages, and was finally possessed by Dark Falz as the final boss of Ep1; her soul is only freed once players defeat DF.

Human maleDeputy commanderThe Ep2 boss himself
Deputy commander of Pioneer 1’s army, a sword saint and Rico’s master, and an old friend of Tyrell . The Flowen's Sword is his signature weapon. He was infected with the Flow parasitic gene while fighting alongside Rico; in Ep2, researchers forcibly fuse his body with D-Photon Core , mutating him into Episode 2’s final boss, Olga Flow.

A new life born when the minds of Rico and Flowen “merge” while forcing Dark Falz and Olga Flow to resonate. It symbolizes the sealing of the two parasites and lays the groundwork for Episode 3’s story.

🕰️ Story timeline

Pioneer 1 lands

Pioneer 1’s advance party lands on Ragol; shortly after sending its “landed successfully” signal, a massive explosion cuts off communication. Rico, Flowen, Osto, Bernie, Zoke and others all went with that ship.

Episode 1 · Forest → Ruins

Pioneer 2 reaches orbit around Ragol and sends Hunters down to investigate. Following the holographic messages Rico left, players uncover the truth: while unsealing the sealing pillars she was possessed by Dark Falz . The Hunters defeat DF and Rico’s soul is freed.

Episode 2 · VR → Seabed

Natasha sends Nol to guide them, and players delve into the seabed lab Pioneer 1 abandoned, discovering that Flowen was implanted with a D-Photon Core and mutated into Olga Flow. In the end, the merged minds of Rico and Flowen force the two parasites to resonate, giving birth to new life, Endu.

Episode 4 · Crater → Subterranean Desert

A meteor strikes Ragol’s surface, forming a crater. Momoka calls on Hunters to investigate, and the research Paganini pushes forward in secret clashes with Natasha’s stance, revealing the government’s persistent tendency to lose control of “new life”.

Episode 3 (C.A.R.D.)· Blue Star Memories

Episode 3 continues the story as a card battle campaign, with characters such as Endu and Break carrying on the legacy of Ep1/Ep2.

Wider setting: the Mother Trinity disaster

After the Pioneer program failed, Coral’s government activated the lunar AI Mother Trinity; it judged humanity a harm to the planet and launched a thermonuclear strike on Coral—a turning point for the whole Phantasy Star setting.
