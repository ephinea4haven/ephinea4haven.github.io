Revelations & reading order (spoilers)

Pioneer 1 establishes a settlement on Ragol. After Pioneer 2 arrives, a huge explosion on the surface cuts off contact. Hunters investigate the lost expedition, altered wildlife and hidden research facilities.

EP1 · Rico / Dark Falz

Following Rico’s records into the ruins reveals that she became Dark Falz’s host. The battle ending and later games’ accounts of her spirit belong to different narrative contexts.

Episode I · Ruins

EP2 · Flowen / Olga Flow

D-cell contamination and experiments involving the AI Olga transform Flowen into Olga Flow. Olga is one of the artificial intelligences associated with the research facilities. Read the island and seabed logs, then explore Elly and Calus in Seat of the Heart.

Episode II · Seabed

EP4 · WORKS / Rupika

Government quests 9-1 through 9-8 follow the missing researchers, meteor and Rupika. Ash, Bernie, Sue, Kireek, Montague and Elenor return alongside Karen, Gilliam and Leo. Some scenes have conditions; one playthrough does not expose every branch.

9-7: Sacred Ground

Further reading: Episode III

Blue Star Memories bridges toward Episode III and features Break, Orland and Nef. Endu’s origins belong to Episode III’s continuation of Rico and Flowen’s story, so he is included as further reading rather than a resident Blue Burst NPC.
