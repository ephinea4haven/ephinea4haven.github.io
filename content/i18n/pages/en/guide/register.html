The registration page includes reCAPTCHA verification, which only appears if you can reach sites outside mainland China:

If reCAPTCHA won’t load (mainland China)

Because Google services are blocked in mainland China, reCAPTCHA on the registration and login pages may fail to load.

A browser extension can redirect the international reCAPTCHA address to a mirror reachable in China:

International address:


https://www.google.com/recaptcha/api.js

China mirror:


https://recaptcha.net/recaptcha/api.js
