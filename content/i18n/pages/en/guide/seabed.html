Male characters

COMBAT

Combat principles

Dolmolm / Dolmdarl

Hell Type/Shot with Hit, plus , is an efficient setup. Androids can freeze them first to lower their EVP, then use Hell.

Deldepth

When several surface at once, drop a Freeze Trap first, then set it off with a first hit that is likely to miss, so they can't use their hit invincibility to dodge the freeze.

Morfos

Turn the camera away from a Morfos before it spawns and keep it off screen to avoid most of its lasers. Double-Morfos waves can't always be blocked this way; keep the one you can't hide on screen and kill it first.

Delbiter

Learn their spawn lines first, then handle them quickly with freezing, Demon's or a high-Machine weapon. Hunters can also use Serene Swan.

Sinow Zoa / Sinow Zele

A high-Machine mechgun can kill them before they act. Turn on Advanced Effect and set Maps graphics to maximum, and the reflections on water and glass show their cloaked pounces.

Recobox / traps

Use Shot's centre lock to hit several targets at once. goes through walls, hits ceiling traps and never misses because of a trap's swaying.
