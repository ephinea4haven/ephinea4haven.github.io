Triggers and Synchro

A Mag’s support effects are governed by its Trigger conditions; their activation chance rises with Synchro and their strength depends on IQ.

Trigger conditions

  • The 10% HP trigger can activate when a character loses more than 20% of their maximum HP in one frame and is left below 10% of maximum HP (for example, ’s special costs 21%).
  • The boss-room trigger is self-explanatory.
  • The 100 PB trigger is self-explanatory.

Synchro

Synchro caps at 120% and drops by 5% each time the character dies. For its effect on Photon Blast power, see the mechanics page’s PB parameter reference.

Mag Synchro rules:

  • +: base chance + tiered bonus
  • −: tiered bonus only
  • None: Synchro is ignored. If a Mag’s trigger effect has no +/−, this rule can be ignored; see Mags )

IQ

IQ affects the Mag’s healing and support effects. For Photon Blast damage, Leilla healing, Mylla & Youlla support levels and chain/donation examples, see Game mechanics: Mag Photon Blasts (PB).
