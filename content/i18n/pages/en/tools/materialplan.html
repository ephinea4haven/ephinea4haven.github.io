Build plans

Early core stats

Prioritise Materials and Mag stats to raise ATP (attack power), ATA (accuracy) and LCK (luck), giving the character their key combat stats at lower levels. This leaves less room for later growth; when approaching level 180 and aiming to cap all stats, you will usually need to reset your Material allocation.

General purpose (maximum stats with minimum units)

Cap final stats with as few units as possible, leaving slots for utility units. Early stats are lower, but all stats can reach their caps later, usually without resetting Materials.

All yellow

Aim to show as many stats as possible in yellow at their caps. Early stats are low, so this is generally not recommended for a fresh character.
